# LEG LIFECYCLE DOCTRINE (LLD v1.0)
### mbappe project · frozen methodology · companion to PSD v1.0 / VTD v1.0

**Status:** PRE-REGISTERED AND FROZEN at commit. Amendment protocol identical to PSD/VTD: registered in writing, with rationale, BEFORE any decision the amendment could affect. Tuning demotion thresholds after watching a live leg struggle is the live-side equivalent of post-hoc criterion change and is prohibited by this doctrine.

**Scope:** governs every strategy leg from validation through retirement. LLD is the live-side extension of the VTD one-way ratchet: promotion is earned by pre-registered evidence; demotion is executed by pre-registered rules; discretion appears nowhere in the loop.

---

## 0. PRINCIPLES

**L-A — Hiring and firing are symmetric obligations.** A validated edge decays (McLean-Pontiff: ~50% post-publication decay is the base rate; live deployment is publication to the market). A lifecycle with promotion rules but no demotion rules is a strategy museum. Every promotion registers its own retirement conditions at the same commit.

**L-B — The moment of decay is the moment of maximum temptation.** When a live leg bleeds, the operator's strongest urge is "give it more time" or "re-tune it." Both are decisions this doctrine removes: the thresholds were chosen when the leg looked good, by the same evidence that promoted it.

**L-C — Only registered, immutable configurations trade.** Live behavior must always be attributable to one pinned config hash. If the running config cannot be attributed, the leg does not trade.

**L-D — Parity precedes promotion.** A leg is promoted on evidence generated by its batch path; the live path is what trades. The two must be proven signal-identical (standing Stage-0 parity harness) before promotion. Live is the source of truth; batch must match it; validation evidence from a divergent batch is void for promotion purposes.

## 1. STATES & ESCALATION LADDER

`CANDIDATE → VALIDATED → PAPER → ACTIVE → WATCH → SAFE_MODE → RETIRED`

| State | Meaning | Entry condition | Trading behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| CANDIDATE | In research pipeline | Registered hypothesis | none |
| VALIDATED | Cleared VTD Stages 0–2 (+PBO) | Registry verdict PASS | none |
| PAPER | In paper soak | Operator go + parity green + config hash pinned | paper orders only |
| ACTIVE | Live capital | Paper criteria met + VTD Stage-4 (SPA, correlation gate) + PAD admission + demotion rules registered | full sizing per PAD budget |
| WATCH | Early-warning tripped | Any Tier-1 monitor (§4) fires | sizing × 0.5; no new instruments; daily DiagnosticReport |
| SAFE_MODE | Demotion tripped | Any Tier-2 monitor fires, or WATCH persists past its registered window | block new entries; manage/flatten existing per risk overlay |
| RETIRED | Permanent exit | Tier-3 condition, or operator ratification of SAFE_MODE review | flat; config archived; ledger closed |

Transitions are executed by the monitor layer and recorded as DiagnosticReports (VTD §4). Manual overrides exist in ONE direction only: the operator may always demote (any state → SAFE_MODE/RETIRED, instantly, no justification needed — the kill switch outranks everything). The operator may never promote past a gate or revive past a tripped monitor without the §5 revival process.

---

## 2. PROMOTION REQUIREMENTS (all gates, no exceptions)

To PAPER: VTD Stages 0–2 pass on the pre-registered criteria; PBO ≤ 0.10; batch↔live parity green for this leg's strategy module; config hash pinned (§3); demotion rule parameters (§4) computed from the leg's own OOS statistics and REGISTERED in the same commit; cost model = captured-spread/realistic tier for the leg's universe (never default bps for small-caps).

To ACTIVE: ≥ the registered paper-soak duration with infra criteria met (charter precedent: PnL is not a promotion criterion; infra correctness is); live-vs-backtest reconciliation within registered divergence bounds (VTD Stage 5); SPA/stepwise survival at the current book level (VTD Stage 4); PAD admission (correlation gate + cluster budget available); holdout shot spent or explicitly reserved, stated in the registry.

---

## 3. CONFIG IMMUTABILITY & HASH LINEAGE

- At promotion to PAPER, the leg's full effective configuration (strategy params, timeframe, risk settings, cost model version, doctrine versions) is serialized canonically and SHA-256 pinned in the registry.
- The live loop asserts running-hash == pinned-hash at startup and on config reload. Mismatch → SAFE_MODE + alert, never silent adoption.
- **Refit lineage (the quarterly-refit interaction, resolved):** the pinned hash covers the leg's STRUCTURE + its L4 adaptation policy (quarterly, neighbor-smoothed/ensemble, per PSD). Parameter values produced BY that registered policy at each refit are legal and produce a new LEAF hash appended to the leg's hash chain: `(leg_id, epoch, parent_hash, leaf_hash, refit_evidence_ref)`. A leaf hash is valid iff generated by the registered refit procedure — verified by the refit runner writing the chain entry itself. Any param change arriving OUTSIDE the refit runner (manual edit, hotfix) invalidates the chain → SAFE_MODE.
- A structural change (signal logic, timeframe, risk rule, cadence) is a NEW LEG VERSION: new registry entry, re-validation through VTD from the appropriate stage. There is no in-place editing of a promoted leg.

---

## 4. DEMOTION MONITORS (registered at promotion, from the leg's own OOS statistics)

All monitors run as non-blocking async tasks off the telemetry stream; every trip writes a DiagnosticReport. Parameters below are the doctrine DEFAULTS — each leg registers its concrete values at promotion; changing them afterward is an amendment.

**Tier 1 — WATCH triggers (early warning):**
1. *Rolling-Sharpe expectation monitor:* 26-week rolling live Sharpe < the leg's OOS bootstrap 95% CI lower bound for 4 consecutive weekly observations. (Both numbers exist at promotion: the CI from VTD Stage 2, the live series from telemetry.)
2. *Cost-divergence monitor:* realized round-trip cost > 1.5× modeled for 10 consecutive trading days (feeds VTD channel (c) recalibration as well).
3. *Hit-rate degradation:* rolling 40-trade hit rate below the OOS hit rate's binomial 95% lower bound.

**Tier 2 — SAFE_MODE triggers (demotion):**
4. *CUSUM drift detector* on weekly live returns: with μ₀, σ = the leg's OOS weekly mean and std, k = 0.5 (half-sigma allowance), h = 5:
   `S_t = max(0, S_{t-1} + (μ₀ − r_t)/σ − k)`; trip when `S_t > h`.
   (One-sided, detects sustained downward drift from the validated mean; standard SPC parameterization, registered per leg.)
5. *Drawdown bound:* live peak-to-trough > 1.25 × the leg's decade-OOS MaxDD. Immediate, no confirmation window.
6. *WATCH persistence:* any Tier-1 condition unresolved for its registered window (default 8 weeks) escalates.
7. *Attribution void:* hash-chain violation, reconciliation mismatch, or parity failure discovered live (these are integrity trips, not performance trips — instant).

**Tier 3 — RETIRED triggers:**
8. SAFE_MODE review (operator, written) ratifies retirement; or the leg's edge family is killed at the registry level by superseding evidence; or two SAFE_MODE episodes within 52 weeks (a leg that keeps tripping is decayed, not unlucky).

**No-discretion clause:** between a monitor trip and its prescribed transition there is no approval step. The operator's freedom is to demote faster, never to hold a tripped leg in place.

---

## 5. REVIVAL

A SAFE_MODE or RETIRED leg re-enters only as a NEW leg version through the front door: fresh pre-registration (quarantine-born if motivated by live diagnostics — VTD §4(a), carrying sibling multiplicity), re-validation on data including the decay period, new promotion, new demotion registration. "It used to work" is not evidence; the decay period is now part of its history and must be survived in-sample.

---

## 6. ADVERSARIAL AUDIT (failure modes of this doctrine)

1. **Threshold gaming at promotion.** Registering absurdly loose demotion parameters (h=50) satisfies the letter while gutting the intent. Mitigation: doctrine defaults are the ceiling of leniency; a leg registering looser-than-default demotion params requires a written amendment with rationale, pre-registered.
2. **Monitor false positives in benign regimes.** A no-signal quarter can starve the rolling windows. Mitigation: monitors 1 and 3 require a registered minimum observation count (weeks with exposure / trades) before they are armed; flat periods extend, not trip.
3. **CUSUM on fat tails.** One −4σ week moves S_t materially. Intended: the DD bound (5) exists precisely for single-event severity; CUSUM is for grinding decay. The two are deliberately redundant in opposite directions.
4. **Hash chain as bureaucracy bypass magnet.** A "quick manual param fix" during an incident is exactly when chain violation is most likely. Mitigation: the incident path is SAFE_MODE (always legal, instant) — never live editing. The runbook (Tier D) encodes this.
5. **Watch-state half-sizing interacting with PAD budgets.** A WATCH leg's freed budget must NOT auto-flow to correlated siblings (that concentrates the same decaying driver). PAD §4 holds freed intra-cluster budget in cash until the leg resolves.
6. **Survivor-only lifecycle statistics.** Retired legs' live histories remain in the ledger and in SPA universes (VTD §5.2) forever. Retirement archives capital, never data.
